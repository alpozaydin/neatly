# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

**neatly collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your files are read and named
on your Mac, with Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). They are never uploaded.

## What stays on your Mac
- The files neatly reads to name them, and the names it suggests.
- Your settings, the names you chose (so neatly can learn your style) and the history of what it renamed (so you can
  undo it). You can erase these any time in neatly → More options → Forget my history.

## The only things that can go online
Never your files or their contents. Each of these can be turned off in neatly's settings:

1. **Research paper titles.** When a downloaded file is an arXiv paper, neatly sends only the paper's number
   (for example `2304.02643`) to arXiv (export.arxiv.org) to get its real title.
2. **Photo places.** For a photo with a location, neatly sends only the two GPS coordinates to Apple's geocoding
   service to get the place name (for example "Rome"). Screenshots are never looked up.
3. **Update check** (only in the version downloaded from the website or Homebrew, not the App Store version).
   Unless you turn it off, neatly asks GitHub every few hours whether there is a newer version.

These services see your IP address like any website does. neatly sends them nothing else about you.

## Folder access
neatly asks for access to your home folder once, so it can name new downloads and screenshots and move files into the
folders you choose. It only renames files with meaningless names, never names you chose, and every change can be
undone.

## Children
neatly is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

On a real Mac

🔒 Private by design

neatly reads your files on your Mac, with Apple's built-in AI (Apple Intelligence). Your files are never uploaded. No account, no tracking.

  • Only three tiny things can go online, never your files: a check for updates, a research paper's number (to find its title), and a photo's location (to name the place).
  • You can turn each of them off in neatly's settings.
  • It's open source: anyone can read exactly what it does.

Will it work on my Mac?

  1. Click the Apple menu (top left) → About This Mac.
  2. Chip should say Apple M1 or newer (M2, M3, M4…). macOS should be 26 or newer.
  3. For the best names, turn on Apple Intelligence: System Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

To remove neatly later: open it → More options → Uninstall neatly.
